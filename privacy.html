PaperPop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version: PaperPop collects nothing and sends nothing. Your notes stay on your computer, and only you can see them.

What PaperPop keeps

PaperPop (called PaperPop Sticky Notes in the Microsoft Store) is a sticky-note app for your desktop. It saves your notes, tasks, drawings and settings in a file on your own computer, plus a few automatic backup copies of that file in the same place. That is all it keeps, and it never leaves your computer.

What PaperPop sends

Nothing. PaperPop:

  • has no account and no sign-in,
  • never connects to the internet (its fonts and sounds are built in),
  • has no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting of its own,
  • does not sell or share any information, because it has none to share.

Export and import

When you choose Export, PaperPop writes your notes to a file in the place you pick. When you choose Import, it reads the file you pick. It does this only when you ask, and the file goes nowhere else.

When you uninstall PaperPop

Your notes live only on your computer, so there is no copy anywhere else. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version can delete them together with the app. Before you uninstall, use Export in PaperPop's Workspace → Settings → Your data, so you can bring them back later.

Other services

  • Microsoft Store and Windows handle downloading, installing and updating PaperPop, and Windows may send diagnostic data to Microsoft according to your Windows settings. This is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement, not by PaperPop.
  • This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may see your IP address when you visit. It uses no cookies or trackers.

Children

PaperPop collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
