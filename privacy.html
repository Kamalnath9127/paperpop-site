PaperPop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version: PaperPop collects nothing and sends nothing. Your notes stay on your computer, and only you can see them. Voice notes turn your speech into text on your computer; your voice is never recorded to a file and never sent anywhere.

What PaperPop keeps

PaperPop (called PaperPop Sticky Notes in the Microsoft Store) is a sticky-note app for your desktop. It saves your notes, tasks, drawings and settings in a file on your own computer, plus a few automatic backup copies of that file in the same place. That is all it keeps, and it never leaves your computer.

What PaperPop sends

Nothing. PaperPop:

  • has no account and no sign-in,
  • works offline (its fonts and sounds are built in); the only time it uses the internet is the one optional voice pack download described below, and only when you ask for it,
  • has no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting of its own,
  • does not sell or share any information, because it has none to share.

Voice notes and the microphone

Voice notes (an Experimental feature, off until you turn it on) let you say tasks instead of typing them.

  • The microphone is used only while you dictate: from the moment you click a note's microphone button (or press Ctrl+Shift+Alt+M) until it stops. While it listens, a red dot shows in the note and the charm wears its listening face. It stops by itself after 10 seconds of silence, after 1 minute, and when you lock or put your computer to sleep.
  • Your voice stays on your computer. The sound is kept only in memory, turned into text on your computer by a built-in speech model (OpenAI's Whisper, running offline), and then thrown away. It is never saved to a file and never sent anywhere. Only the text becomes a task in your note.
  • To recognise names you use, PaperPop looks at the words in your notes and in your Words I use list in Settings. This also happens only on your computer.
  • The voice pack: the speech model (about 60 MB) is not part of the app. PaperPop downloads it once, only after you turn voice notes on, from Hugging Face (where the speech model is published) or, if that does not work, from PaperPop's own page on GitHub. Nothing about you or your notes is sent with that download; like any download, Hugging Face and GitHub can see your IP address (Hugging Face Privacy Policy, GitHub Privacy Statement). You can delete the voice pack any time in Settings.
  • Windows decides whether apps may use the microphone; you can turn it off for PaperPop in Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone.

Send feedback

The Send feedback button in Settings opens your own email app with a new message to PaperPop. The only thing PaperPop adds is its version number; no recordings and no notes. You read the message and decide whether to send it.

Export and import

When you choose Export, PaperPop writes your notes to a file in the place you pick. When you choose Import, it reads the file you pick. It does this only when you ask, and the file goes nowhere else.

When you uninstall PaperPop

Your notes live only on your computer, so there is no copy anywhere else. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version can delete them together with the app. Before you uninstall, use Export in PaperPop's Workspace → Settings → Your data, so you can bring them back later.

Other services

  • Microsoft Store and Windows handle downloading, installing and updating PaperPop, and Windows may send diagnostic data to Microsoft according to your Windows settings. This is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement, not by PaperPop.
  • This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may see your IP address when you visit. It uses no cookies or trackers.

Children

PaperPop collects no information from anyone, including children.
